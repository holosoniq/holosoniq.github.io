The delivery problem

Promising CNS therapies need a better route to the target.

Many promising CNS programs are limited less by therapeutic concept than by delivery. The broader vision is a universal brain-access infrastructure layer for drugs, biologics, gene therapies, cell therapies, and next-generation neurotherapeutics.

How it works

Three technology pillars, one deployable workflow.

01

Acoustic holography

3D-printed holographic lenses are designed for high-fidelity volumetric beam shaping for distributed and multifocal treatment volumes.

02

Predictive control

Machine-learning models use passive cavitation signals to anticipate microbubble instability and support safer, more reproducible blood-brain barrier opening.

03

MRI-free navigation

Nonlinear acoustic feedback, optical registration, and robotic positioning are being integrated into a streamlined translational workflow.
