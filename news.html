September 2026 cohort

Selected for HBS Foundry Catalyst

HoloSoniQ founder Costas was selected for Harvard Business School's Foundry Catalyst September 2026 cohort, joining 30 founders chosen from a competitive pool of more than 1,000 applicants.

The intensive four-week program offers a dedicated venture mentor, weekly small-group sessions with Harvard faculty and investors, and access to experts and advisors. Participants also have an opportunity to pitch for $100,000 in non-dilutive funding.
